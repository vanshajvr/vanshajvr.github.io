PRODUCT · ML EVALUATION

Parakh↗

Stock signals with receipts, for beginner Indian investors.

Problem
Beginners act on tips from Telegram groups and finfluencers that carry no track record, confidence or reasoning.
Result
A tuned LightGBM didn't beat a one-line reversal rule. On a held-out 2025–26 test, neither beat a coin flip, so the product competes on honesty instead: calibrated leans, "No call", and a public ledger nobody can edit.
Key decision
Rules written down before testing, and the simpler model shipped. Choosing LightGBM on the only clean test would have been picking on luck.

What drives Delhi's air pollution?↗

Government monitoring data joined to weather and satellite fire data.

Question
How much of Delhi's winter smog is weather, how much is crop burning, and can a simple rule warn before a bad-air day?
Result
Winter smog is mostly shallow winter air. Crop burning adds up to 2.2× beyond what the weather predicts. One early-warning rule caught 69% of first bad days on years it was never tuned on.
Key decision
Test plans pre-registered before running the numbers, and failures reported in the same table as passes.
